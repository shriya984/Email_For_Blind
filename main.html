EMAIL SYSTEM FOR BLIND PEOPLE

A speech-based email system designed for visually impaired users to send, receive, and manage emails using only voice commands.

🔊 Built with speech recognition & text-to-speech, BlindMail allows users to compose, read, and delete emails fully through voice without keyboard or mouse.

Our Features

VOICE FEEDBACK

System gives audio response after each action to guide the user.

Helps blind users understand what's happening on screen without seeing it.

SPEECH COMMANDS

Control everything with voice: reading, composing, deleting emails.

Just say “Read inbox” or “Compose email” and let the system do the rest.

ACCESSIBILITY FIRST

Designed specially with visually impaired users in mind.

Big buttons, minimal visuals, full audio guidance — no screen reader needed!

How It Works

1. Speak

User gives command: "Compose Email" or "Read Inbox".

2. AI Processes

Speech-to-Text converts voice into email actions.

3. System Responds

Text-to-Speech reads out inbox and confirms actions.

About Us

BlindMail is developed to empower visually impaired individuals with a fully accessible email system. Our mission is to remove digital communication barriers by providing speech-based technology for independent emailing.
